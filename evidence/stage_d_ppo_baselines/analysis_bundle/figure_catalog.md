# Stage-D figure catalog

## Figure 1 — Sharpe contrasts

Files: `stage_d_sharpe_contrasts.png` and `.pdf`. Diamonds show mean Sharpe differences across six dataset-window cells; bars show 95% hierarchical circular-block-bootstrap intervals (block=20, resamples=10,000). Positive values favor the first named method.

## Figure 2 — Absolute Sharpe heatmap

Files: `stage_d_absolute_sharpe_heatmap.png` and `.pdf`. Each tile is the mean out-of-sample Sharpe across three seeds for one method and one dataset-window cell. It prevents favorable pairwise contrasts from hiding poor absolute performance or failure to beat Equal Weight.
